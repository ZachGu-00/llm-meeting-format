Definition and symbol ambiguity
The v3 proposal line231 states a positive Spearman association between |C| and U.
Its line110 calls U the residual fraction and inherits v2 section8, whose line471
defines U=sum((DeltaO-DeltaP0)^2)/sum(DeltaO^2). However, v3 appendix line275 also
uses U(H,H') for the signed residual of an individual design pair. These are
different quantities. Do not silently rename the residual RMS as the fraction.

Primary H3 is one Spearman correlation across the six selected models between:
  x_m = abs(C_m), from the already specified primary E1 R0 analysis;
  y_m = U_m, the E4b single-round normalized unexplained fraction.
For each model and topic, retain all six initial configurations and twenty
shared mappings. Contrast all pairs of the eighty STS15 designs within each
common mapping/configuration before aggregation. Let a_mt be the mean squared
P0 residual and d_mt the mean squared observed contrast in that topic. Then
  U_m = mean_over_six_topics(a_mt) / mean_over_six_topics(d_mt).
This is exactly aggregate.single_round.E4b.metrics.P0_unexplained_fraction_U,
not the mean of six topic ratios and not a signed mapping-averaged difference.
The separately labelled sensitivity uses sqrt(mean_over_topics(a_mt)), exactly
P0_residual_RMS. E4a/E4c and dynamic correlations are not additional primary H3
tests in this clarification. No outcome-driven choice among them is permitted.

Primary C remains fresh180 for Qwen3.5-4B and LFM2.5-1.2B, and full200 for other
selected models. Do not replace it with the adaptive E9 balanced-old20 result.
Preserve the existing matched44/64 R0/R1 diagnostics as readout qualifications;
this H3 does not imply that E4 R1 caches or free dialogue have been measured.

Uncertainty and inference boundaries
Use10000 topic-cluster bootstrap draws. In each draw all six models share the
same E1 topic multiplicities and, independently, the same E4 topic multiplicities.
Recompute each model's C from its own item_C_numerators/item_C_denominators and
topic labels, preserving its specified180/200 cohort. Recompute each U as the
ratio of resampled topic error/contrast components, then compute Spearman rho.
Align by actual topic IDs; never concatenate independent per-model bootstrap
rows unless their weights and topic order have first been proven identical.
The measurement interval is conditional on this selected model panel and fixed
within-topic caches/designs; it is not a random-LLM-population confidence interval.
Retain undefined draws, zero denominators, ties and constant-variable cases
explicitly; do not discard a model or resample until a favorable result appears.
Report each of the six leave-one-model-out correlations as small-panel sensitivity.

For the single primary positive-association hypothesis, report the inclusive
one-sided permutation p from all6!=720 assignments of model labels to one of
the two observed vectors, with ties handled by the same Spearman definition.
This is a conditional selected-panel permutation diagnostic under exchangeable
labels, not proof of random model sampling, architecture independence or a
population/causal effect. Report a negative or undefined association plainly.
There is one primary H3 correlation, not six separate model hypotheses. The
existing H1 two-hypothesis Holm family is unchanged. The residual-RMS sensitivity
is descriptive; it creates no additional primary test family. No new scientific
success threshold or positive-result requirement is introduced.
